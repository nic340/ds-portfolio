Course: DS Elective 4 - Deep Learning

1. Descriptive#

  • How many vaccine doses have been distributed in the Philippines in the past month?

  • What is the current vaccination rate across different regions?

2. Diagnostic#

  • Why did vaccine production slow down in the last quarter?

  • What factors contributed to the lower vaccination turnout in rural areas?

3. Predictive#

  • How many additional vaccine doses will be needed in the next three months?

  • What is the projected infection rate if 70% of the population is vaccinated by year-end?

4. Prescriptive#

  • What distribution strategy should be implemented to ensure remote areas receive vaccines on time?

  • How should resources be allocated to maximize vaccination coverage while minimizing costs?
